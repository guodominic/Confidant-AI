智己 Confidant AI 使用条款

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. 服务说明

智己（Confidant AI）是一款 iOS + Apple Watch 语音记录与反思应用，提供录音、转写、摘要与 AI 分析功能。不订阅时只能录音和使用设备上的功能；Pro（$9.99/月）与 Max（$19.99/月）为自动续订订阅（也可按年订阅，年费等于 10 个月），提供云转写和 AI 分析的云端额度。额度用完后可以购买一次性额度包，额度包只能在有效订阅期内使用。

Confidant AI is an iOS + Apple Watch voice journaling and reflection app offering recording, transcription, summarization and AI analysis. Without a subscription you can record and use on-device features; Pro ($9.99/mo) and Max ($19.99/mo) are auto-renewing subscriptions (also available yearly, priced at 10 months) with cloud quota for transcription and AI analysis. When the quota runs out you can buy one-time credit packs, usable only during an active subscription.

2. 订阅与计费

订阅通过 Apple App Store 计费，自动续订，除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消。Pro 可享 14 天免费试用（试用期云端额度有限，深度思考不可用）。取消后录音和已有资料保留，但云端功能停用。额度包退款后，未使用的额度会被收回。价格可能调整，以 App Store 显示为准。

Subscriptions are billed via the Apple App Store and auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Pro includes a 14-day free trial (limited cloud quota; deep thinking isn't available during the trial). After cancellation your recordings and existing data stay, but cloud features stop. If a credit pack is refunded, its unused credits are removed. Prices may change; the App Store listing governs.

You must not: (a) record content you have no right to record; (b) use the app for any unlawful purpose; (c) reverse-engineer or disrupt the service. Comply with recording consent laws in your jurisdiction (e.g. one-party/two-party consent rules).

Transcriptions, summaries and AI analyses are algorithmically generated and may be inaccurate or incomplete. They are for reference only and do not constitute professional (medical, legal, financial, etc.) advice. Verify important information independently.
