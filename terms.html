最后更新：2026年10月6日

1. 服务说明

知己（ConfidantAI）是一款 iOS + Apple Watch 语音记录与反思应用，提供录音、转写、摘要与 AI 分析功能。标准版（Standard）免费；Pro（$9.99/月）与 Max（$19.99/月）为自动续订订阅，提供云转写额度与 AI 分析功能。

ConfidantAI is an iOS + Apple Watch voice journaling and reflection app offering recording, transcription, summarization and AI analysis. The Standard tier is free; Pro ($9.99/mo) and Max ($19.99/mo) are auto-renewing subscriptions with cloud transcription quota and AI analysis.

2. 订阅与计费

订阅通过 Apple App Store（StoreKit 2）计费，自动续订，除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消。取消后已下载的模型保留，但 Pro/Max 功能锁定。价格可能调整，以 App Store 显示为准。

Subscriptions are billed via the Apple App Store (StoreKit 2) and auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. After cancellation, downloaded models are kept but Pro/Max features are locked. Prices may change; the App Store listing governs.
