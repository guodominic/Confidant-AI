智己 Confidant AI 隐私政策

Confidant AI Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

概要 Summary

智己帮你回顾自己的对话。录音一律由你亲自开始，App 不会偷偷录音。你的录音、转写和整理出的资料保存在你的 iPhone 上。只有在你明确同意之后，App 才会把转写和分析需要的最少内容，经我们的服务器发给第三方云端 AI 服务商处理。我们不出售你的数据，不做广告，不用你的内容训练模型。

Confidant helps you reflect on your own conversations. Recording only ever starts when you start it; the app never records secretly. Your recordings, transcripts and the information organized from them are stored on your iPhone. Only after you explicitly agree does the app send the minimum content needed for transcription and analysis, through our server, to third-party cloud AI providers. We don't sell your data, show ads, or train models on your content.

1. 保存在你手机上的数据 Data on your device

  • 录音、转写、摘要、分析、「关于我」、知识层、聊天记录、承诺和每周回顾。
    Recordings, transcripts, summaries, analyses, About me, the knowledge layer, chats, commitments and weekly reviews.
  • 声纹特征（你自己的，以及经对方同意后学习的他人声音）：存在你的设备上；只有你打开「iCloud 同步」时，才会同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，供你登录同一个 Apple ID 的其他设备使用。我们和云端 AI 服务商都拿不到。
    Voiceprint features (yours, and other people's only with their consent): stored on your devices; only if you turn on iCloud sync are they synced through your own private iCloud to your other devices on the same Apple ID. Neither we nor the cloud AI providers can access them.
  • iCloud 同步（默认关闭）：打开后，录音的文字资料、聊天、「关于我」、人物和声纹通过你自己的 iCloud 私有空间在你的设备之间同步；音频不同步。数据由 Apple 保存在你的 iCloud 里，我们看不到。
    iCloud sync (off by default): when on, recording text data, chats, About me, people and voiceprints sync between your devices through your own private iCloud; audio does not sync. Apple stores it in your iCloud; we cannot see it.
  • 原始录音默认在转写后 24 小时删除，你可以在录音选项里改成其他期限；转写和资料保留到你自己删除。
    Original audio is deleted 24 hours after transcription by default (changeable in Recording options); transcripts and other data are kept until you delete them.
  • 如果你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 云备份，这些数据会包含在你自己的备份里。
    If iCloud Backup is on, this data is included in your own device backup.

2. 发给云端的数据 Data sent to the cloud

只有在你于 App 内点「同意」之后才会发送。
Sent only after you tap "Agree" in the app.

  • 转写 Transcription — AssemblyAI（美国 US）：音频先在手机上剪掉静音和非人声，再经我们的服务器上传转写。转写完成后，我们会让 AssemblyAI 立即删除音频和结果。
    Audio is first trimmed on your phone to remove silence and non-speech, then uploaded through our server. When transcription finishes, we have AssemblyAI delete the audio and transcript right away.
  • 摘要、分析与对话 Summaries, analysis & chat — OpenAI（美国 US）：订阅或试用时，每条录音转写完成后，完整的转写文字会发给 OpenAI 写摘要、智己分析，并整理「关于我」和知识层；转写片段和你的搜索词也会发给 OpenAI 计算向量，用于语义搜索，索引存在你的手机上；定期回顾和人物画像也发给 OpenAI 生成；聊天时会发送录音目录（标题、日期、出现的人、话题）以及和问题相关的录音转写、你在「关于我」里的事实和你的问题。不订阅时，摘要由 Apple 智能在你的设备上写，不发给 OpenAI。OpenAI 默认不用 API 数据训练模型，可能为防滥用保留最多 30 天。
    With a subscription or trial, once a recording is transcribed its full transcript is sent to OpenAI to write the summary and analysis and to update About me and the knowledge layer. Transcript passages and your search terms are also sent to OpenAI to compute embeddings for meaning search; the index stays on your phone. Periodic reviews and people portraits are also generated by OpenAI. In chat we send a catalog of your recordings (titles, dates, people, topics) plus the transcripts relevant to your question, facts in About me, and your question. Without a subscription, summaries are written on your device by Apple Intelligence and are not sent to OpenAI. By default OpenAI doesn't train on API data and may retain it up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • 日历和健康 Calendar & Health（只有你允许了才读 only if you allow access）：你在聊天里问到日程时，那段时间的日程（时间、标题、地点）会和问题一起发给 OpenAI；只有你确认后才会往日历里添加日程。Apple 健康里录音那段时间的心率和 HRV 只在手机上显示，只有你点「问智己」时才会写进那条问题发给 OpenAI。
    When you ask about your schedule in chat, events in that period (time, title, location) are sent to OpenAI with your question; events are added to your calendar only after you confirm. Heart rate and HRV from Apple Health for the time of a recording stay on your phone, and go into a question to OpenAI only when you tap Ask.
  • 不会发给我们或 AI 服务商 Never sent to us or AI providers：声纹特征、通讯录、位置。
    Voiceprint features, contacts, location.

3. 我们服务器上的数据 Data on our server

我们的服务器（Cloudflare）负责转发请求、确认订阅、记录用量和登录账户，并短暂保存云端结果，让 App 在后台或断线后还能取回：
Our server (Cloudflare) relays requests, verifies subscriptions, tracks usage and handles sign-in, and briefly keeps cloud results so the app can collect them after being in the background or offline:

  • 不保存音频。转写结果和摘要结果在你的手机取回后 1 小时内删除（没取回的最长 24 小时）；聊天回答写完后保留 1 小时。
    No audio is stored. Transcripts and summaries are deleted within an hour after your phone collects them (24 hours at most if never collected); chat answers are kept for one hour after they're written.
  • 订阅交易编号（向 Apple 核实订阅）、本期云端用量金额、随机生成的设备编号（防止滥用，一个订阅最多 5 台设备）、推送编号（转写完成时叫醒 App，不显示内容）。不保存你的姓名和付款信息。
    Your subscription transaction ID (to verify with Apple), this period's cloud usage amount, a randomly generated device ID (abuse prevention; up to 5 devices per subscription) and a push token (to wake the app when transcription finishes; it shows nothing). We don't store your name or payment details.
  • 账户（可选，只有你登录才有）：邮箱、Apple / Google 账号标识、用来撤销 Apple 登录的凭证、邀请码和额度包记录。邮箱验证码通过 Resend 发送。在 设置 ▸ 账户 里可以删除账户，账户和登录方式会被删除；只留下不含邮箱的额度记录，用于防止重复兑换。
    Account (optional, only if you sign in): email, Apple / Google account identifiers, a credential used to revoke Sign in with Apple, your invite code and credit-pack records. Email codes are sent through Resend. You can delete your account in Settings ▸ Account; the account and sign-in methods are removed, leaving only credit records without your email to prevent duplicate redemption.
  • 如果你填了自己的 OpenAI / AssemblyAI 密钥：密钥存在手机的钥匙串里，每次请求经过我们的服务器转发给对方，我们不保存、不记录。
    If you add your own OpenAI / AssemblyAI keys: they stay in your phone's keychain and pass through our server with each request; we don't store or log them.
  • 日志只有错误代码和用量数字，不含录音、转写或聊天内容。
    Logs contain only error codes and usage numbers, never recordings, transcripts or chats.

4. 诊断信息 Diagnostics

App 用 Apple 的 MetricKit 在你的手机上记录崩溃和卡死信息（不含任何内容），不会自动上传；只有你在 设置 ▸ 关于智己 AI ▸ 诊断信息 里点「分享」才会发出。
The app uses Apple's MetricKit to record crashes and hangs on your phone (no content). Nothing is uploaded automatically; it's only sent if you tap Share in Settings ▸ About Confidant AI ▸ Diagnostics.

匿名使用统计：App 会把事件名（例如“录音已保存”“打开了分析”）、大致时长的分段（例如“10–60 分钟”）、App 版本和“安装后第几天”发给我们的服务器，用来了解功能是否好用。不包含录音、文字、标题、人名，也不带账户或设备编号。可以在 设置 ▸ 数据与隐私 ▸ 匿名使用统计 里关闭。

Anonymous usage stats: the app sends event names (such as "recording saved" or "analysis opened"), rough duration ranges (such as "10–60 min"), the app version and days since install to our server, to learn whether features work well. They contain no audio, text, titles or names, and no account or device ID. Turn them off in Settings ▸ Data & privacy ▸ Anonymous usage stats.

5. 录别人 Recording others

录别人之前，请先告诉对方并征得同意；有些地方的法律要求所有参与者都同意。学习别人的声音之前，App 会请你确认对方已经同意。
Tell people and get their consent before recording them; some places require everyone in a conversation to agree. Before learning someone else's voice, the app asks you to confirm they agreed.

人物画像：订阅后，App 可以根据你和某个人的对话，用 OpenAI 写一份只给你看的画像（沟通方式、在意的事、习惯等；如果是孩子，会写兴趣、性格特点和成长）。它只根据录音内容，不做心理或医学诊断、不贴标签。画像保存在你的手机上（开了 iCloud 同步时也在你的 iCloud 里），你可以删除。
People portraits: with a subscription, the app can use OpenAI to write a portrait, visible only to you, of someone you talk with (communication style, what they care about, habits; for a child, interests, personality traits and growth). It's based only on your recordings, with no psychological or medical diagnosis and no labels. Portraits are stored on your phone (and in your iCloud if sync is on), and you can delete them.

6. 你的权利 Your choices

  • 随时导出：设置 ▸ 数据与隐私 ▸ 导出我的数据。
    Export anytime: Settings ▸ Data & privacy ▸ Export my data.
  • 随时删除：删除一条录音会连带删除从它整理出的全部内容；设置 ▸ 删除全部本地数据 会清空手机上的一切，并退出账户。账户要在 设置 ▸ 账户 里单独删除。
    Delete anytime: deleting a recording also deletes everything derived from it; Settings ▸ Delete all local data clears everything on your phone and signs you out. Delete your account separately in Settings ▸ Account.
  • 不想再用云端处理：删除全部本地数据、删除账户，或卸载 App。之后不会再发送任何内容。
    To stop using cloud processing: delete all local data, delete your account, or uninstall the app. Nothing more is sent after that.

7. 订阅 Subscriptions

订阅由 Apple App Store 处理，我们拿不到你的付款信息。
Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details.

8. 儿童 Children

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
The app is not directed to children under 13.

9. 政策更新 Changes

政策有实质变化时，App 会再次请你确认同意。
If this policy changes materially, the app will ask for your consent again.

10. 联系我们 Contact

隐私问题请通过 App Store 上的开发者页面联系我们。
For privacy questions, contact us via the developer page on the App Store.
