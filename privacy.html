知己 ConfidantAI 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年10月6日

1. 我们收集什么

录音与转写文本：您主动录制的内容。标准版转写在设备端完成；Pro/Max 的云转写会将音频片段发送至我们的转写服务提供商（AssemblyAI）处理。声纹模型数据仅存储在您的设备上，永不上传。

Recordings & transcripts: Content you choose to record. Standard-tier transcription runs on-device; Pro/Max cloud transcription sends audio segments to our transcription provider (AssemblyAI). Voiceprint model data never leaves your device.

账户与订阅：通过 Apple App Store 处理的订阅状态，我们不存储您的支付卡信息。

Account & subscription: Subscription status via the Apple App Store; we do not store your payment card details.

诊断数据：匿名的崩溃与性能数据，用于改进应用。

Diagnostics: Anonymous crash and performance data used to improve the app.

2. 数据如何使用

您的数据仅用于提供、维护和改进本应用功能（如转写、摘要、AI 分析）。我们不出售您的个人信息，不将您的录音内容用于广告。

Your data is used solely to provide, maintain and improve app features (transcription, summarization, AI analysis). We do not sell personal information and do not use your recordings for advertising.

3. 数据保留

录音文件在转写完成后 24 小时内自动删除；资料库记录在 7 天后彻底自动删除；您可随时在应用内手动删除，手动删除会同步清除其在归档中的条目。

Recordings auto-delete within 24 hours after transcription; library records are permanently auto-deleted after 7 days. You may delete manually at any time; manual deletion also removes its archive entries.

4. 第三方服务

Pro/Max 云转写使用 AssemblyAI（音频片段传输与处理）；AI 分析使用云端大语言模型。适用第三方各自的隐私政策。我们要求服务商仅按指示处理数据。

Pro/Max cloud transcription uses AssemblyAI (audio segment transfer and processing); AI analysis uses cloud LLMs. Third parties' own privacy policies apply. Providers process data only on our instructions.

5. 您的权利

您可以随时在应用内查看、导出或删除您的数据。删除 Apple ID 相关数据请按 Apple 官方流程操作。

You may view, export or delete your data in the app at any time. For Apple-ID-related data deletion, follow Apple's official process.

6. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。如发现误收集儿童信息，请联系我们删除。

The app is not directed to children under 13. If such data was collected inadvertently, contact us for deletion.

7. 政策更新

我们可能更新本政策，重大变更将在应用内提示。继续使用即表示接受更新后的政策。

We may update this policy; material changes will be notified in-app. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

8. 联系我们

隐私问题请通过 App Store 开发者页面联系。

For privacy questions, contact us via the App Store developer page.
